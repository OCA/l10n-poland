Poland - Elixir-O payment files (with split payment)

Generates Elixir-O files from payment orders. Elixir-O is a common format for importing domestic PLN transfers into Polish business e-banking. Files made with this format were imported successfully in a Polish cooperative bank; banks differ in details, so check your bank’s import description before the first real payment.

The module also supports the Polish split payment mechanism (MPP, mechanizm podzielonej płatności): the transfer gets the /VAT/…/IDC/…/INV/… message and transaction code 53, so the bank sends the VAT part to the supplier’s VAT account.

Split payment follows the invoice. For vendor bills imported from KSeF (module l10n_pl_edi) the flag is taken from the invoice (field P_18A, “mechanizm podzielonej płatności”), and the supplier bank account from the invoice payment details (NrRB). The module never ticks split payment by the amount alone.

Configuration

  1. Set the bank account (NRB/IBAN) on your bank journal and add the payment method Elixir-O (Polish domestic transfers) to its outgoing methods.
  2. Create a payment mode with this payment method (Invoicing > Configuration > Payment Modes).
  3. Suppliers need a bank account; split payment also needs their Polish NIP.
  4. Users who import KSeF bills need the Payments group of account_payment_order to add a supplier bank account found on the invoice. Without it the bill is still imported, only without the account.

Usage

  1. Vendor bills imported from KSeF come with Split payment (MPP) ticked when the supplier marked the invoice with split payment. A bank account found on the invoice that the supplier did not use before is added as not trusted, so check it before you pay.
  2. For other bills tick Split payment (MPP) yourself. A PLN bill above 15 000 PLN gross with VAT from a Polish supplier shows a reminder to check whether split payment applies (it is mandatory for goods and services from annex 15 to the VAT act).
  3. Add bills to a payment order, confirm it and click Generate payment file. A split payment transfer always pays one invoice; for a partial payment the VAT part is proportional and can be edited on the payment line.
  4. Import the file in your bank’s e-banking and authorize the transfers there. The bank authorization (token, mobile app or qualified signature) stays with you; Odoo never sends money by itself.
