This module adds `Enable Banking <https://enablebanking.com>`__ as an
online bank statements provider. Enable Banking gives read access to
bank accounts through the PSD2 account information APIs of many European
banks. The module was tested with Bank Millennium (Poland) and Revolut;
the list of banks shown in the wizard comes from Enable Banking.

One *Connect bank* button guides you through the Enable Banking
application, lets you pick the bank, and creates a bank journal for
every account the bank returns. Transactions are then pulled on schedule
into bank statements, without any file import. Your bank login never
reaches Odoo: you authorize the access on the bank's own page, and the
consent is valid for up to 180 days (depending on the bank).

The module uses its own technical names (service
``l10n_pl_enablebanking``, fields ``l10n_pl_eb_*``, callback
``/l10n_pl_enablebanking/callback``), so it can be installed next to the
independent ``account_statement_import_online_enablebanking`` module by
Futural and RockIT.

Configuration
=============

Everything is done from *Invoicing > Bank > Connect bank* (also the
*Connect bank* button on a bank tile of the dashboard, or *Add bank* in
its menu).

The wizard first asks whether you already have an Enable Banking
application.

**No:** it shows the steps:

1. Create a free account at https://enablebanking.com/sign-in/.
2. In *API applications* register an application: *Production* for your
   real accounts or *Sandbox* for test banks; keep the default key
   option; as *Allowed redirect URL* paste the address shown in the
   wizard (``https://<your-odoo>/l10n_pl_enablebanking/callback``).
   Production also requires a Privacy URL and Terms URL.
3. Production: click *Activate by linking accounts* next to the
   application and link your accounts in every bank (restricted mode).
   Permanent use in a company requires a contract with Enable Banking.

**Yes:** upload the downloaded ``.pem`` file. Its name is the
application ID, which is filled in automatically. Odoo checks the key
with Enable Banking and stores it on the company (accounting managers
only). It is entered once; it can be changed later in *Settings > Bank &
Cash > Enable Banking*.

Then choose the bank, log in on the bank page and confirm. Back in Odoo
a bank journal is created for every account returned by the bank (name,
IBAN, currency) and the last 30 days are downloaded. A journal that
already has the same IBAN is reused; the first account fills an unused
bank journal (no IBAN, no statement lines yet, e.g. the default *Bank*)
instead of creating a new one.

The user who connects the bank must be an accounting manager with full
accounting rights (the *Accounting: Full access* level, which can create
bank statements). With invoicing rights only the connection is made, but
the first download fails with an access error shown in the provider's
chatter.

Usage
=====

The *Connect bank* wizard lists the banks of the company's country. Tick
*Banks from all countries* to see every bank available through Enable
Banking, for example Revolut or an account held abroad; the bank's
country is shown in the list and used for the consent.

New transactions are downloaded by the scheduled action *Pull Online
Bank Statements*, or at once with *Download now* on the bank tile of the
dashboard.

*Bank > Bank connections* lists every bank consent with its accounts and
expiry date. Seven days before the consent expires a message is posted
on the connection; *Renew consent* logs in to the bank again and
*Disconnect* ends the access (journals and statements stay).

*Bank > To reconcile* opens the reconciliation screen of
``account_reconcile_oca`` when it is installed, otherwise a list of
unreconciled bank lines.
